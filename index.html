Virtual Event Planning & Operations System

USAID · AI-Assisted Workflow Design

Designed a reusable, end-to-end planning system for virtual events, using ChatGPT as a design partner to structure and refine a complex event-management workflow.

The system uses a multi-tab Google Doc to keep logistics, communications, event/presentation materials, and event records together in one shared workspace. Smart-chips are utilized throughout to easily insert organization names, dates and calendar invites. The system is designed to be collaborative and reusable, with clear ownership and handoffs for each step of the event planning process.

My Role AI-assisted process design · centralized operations · clear ownership and handoffs · reusable workflow
